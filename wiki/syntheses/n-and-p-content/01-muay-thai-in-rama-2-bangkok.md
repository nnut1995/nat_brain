# 01 — Muay Thai in Rama 2, Bangkok

## Content brief

- Week: Week 1
- Goal: Awareness
- Format: Reel, 15–25 seconds
- Target audience: Rama 2 locals, Bangkok fitness beginners, nearby office workers
- Main offer: Trial class from 199 THB
- Hook: Looking for real Muay Thai training in Rama 2?

## Concept

Introduce N&P as a local Rama 2 Muay Thai camp with beginner-to-pro training.

## What to shoot / create

- Exterior/sign or establishing shot of the gym.
- Trainer holding pads.
- Beginner or student hitting pads.
- Quick group class atmosphere shot.
- End card: Rama 2, start with 199 THB THB, Line @ppaoin.

## What Nat has to do

1. Shoot vertical video in daylight or during a lively class.
2. Capture 5–7 clips, each 2–4 seconds long.
3. Add text overlay: Muay Thai in Rama 2, Bangkok.
4. Show price only once at the end: Regular class from 400 THB.
5. Post to IG Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, then pin if it performs well.

## Caption draft

Muay Thai training in Rama 2, Bangkok. Beginner-friendly, fighter-led, and open to all levels. Message us to book your first class.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Local Beginner Awareness. Target 3–8 km around Rama 2; interests: Muay Thai, boxing, fitness, weight loss. Objective: Messages. Budget: 300–500 THB/day for 7 days.

## CTA

Message us on Line @ppaoin to book your first class.


## Source facts to keep consistent

- N&P Boxing Gym / N&P Muaythai Camp is in Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class pricing: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is 90 minutes and available in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

> [!warning]
> Avoid overpromising: the vault notes flag incomplete full address details, ambiguity around staycation booking/inclusions, and a holiday-opening contradiction between the website and Facebook source ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).

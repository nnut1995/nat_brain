# 05 — Trainer Pad Work Reel

## Content brief

- Week: Week 1
- Goal: Credibility
- Format: Reel, 10–20 seconds
- Target audience: Beginners plus serious trainees
- Main offer: Fighter-led training
- Hook: This is what real pad work looks like at N&P.

## Concept

Show energy, rhythm, and trainer expertise through pad work.

## What to shoot / create

- Close-up of trainer holding pads.
- Student/fighter hitting combinations.
- Slow-motion kick or knee.
- Trainer correction moment.
- End card with Line and phone.

## What Nat has to do

1. Film stable vertical close-ups.
2. Use natural gym sound if strong; otherwise add energetic music.
3. Keep cuts fast but readable.
4. Overlay: Fighter-led Muay Thai training.
5. Post as Reel and reuse for ads.

## Caption draft

Pad work, timing, power, and technique — train the N&P way in Rama 2.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Proof / Credibility. Target Muay Thai, boxing, combat sports, MMA interests. Objective: Video Views or Messages. Use as top-of-funnel creative.

## CTA

Message us to try a class.

- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).

- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

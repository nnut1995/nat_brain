# 18 — Clinch, Sparring, and Fight IQ

## Content brief

- Week: Week 4
- Goal: Advanced credibility
- Format: Reel, 15–30 seconds
- Target audience: Advanced students and serious trainees
- Main offer: Staycation/pro program or private training
- Hook: More than fitness: timing, clinch, sparring, fight IQ.

## Concept

Show advanced/pro training without scaring beginners.

## What to shoot / create

- Technical sparring clip.
- Clinch drill.
- Pad work with advanced combo.
- Trainer coaching ring strategy.
- End card: advanced/pros welcome.

## What Nat has to do

1. Only film safe, controlled technical work.
2. Add text: advanced/pro training available.
3. Avoid overly violent clips for ad approval.
4. Use organic first; if boosting, keep copy technical and non-aggressive.
5. Route serious leads to private/staycation.

## Caption draft

For advanced students and serious trainees: pad work, clinching, technical sparring, and fight IQ.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Advanced Muay Thai. Target Muay Thai, MMA, combat sports, fight camp interests. Objective: Messages; likely smaller but higher-intent audience.

## CTA

Message us about private training or staycation.

- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

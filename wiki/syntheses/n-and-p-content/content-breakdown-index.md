## Execution rule

For each content item:

1. Shoot or design the required creative.
2. Post organically first, unless it is a direct offer post.
3. If the post gets strong saves, shares, comments, or watch time, reuse it as an ad.
4. For direct offer posts, run message ads immediately with a small test budget.
5. Track cost per message, bookings, and actual attendance.

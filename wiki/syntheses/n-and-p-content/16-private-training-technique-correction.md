# 16 — Private Training Technique Correction

## Content brief

- Week: Week 4
- Goal: Private training conversion
- Format: Reel, 20–35 seconds
- Target audience: People who want faster progress
- Main offer: Private training from 700 THB
- Hook: Small correction. Big improvement.

## Concept

Show why private training is valuable: the trainer spots and fixes technique.

## What to shoot / create

- Student throws technique before correction.
- Trainer explains/corrects.
- Student repeats improved version.
- Side-by-side or before/after text.
- End card: private 700 THB.

## What Nat has to do

1. Choose one simple technique correction.
2. Make the improvement visible.
3. Keep trainer audio if clear.
4. Add captions for silent viewing.
5. Run as private training ad.

## Caption draft

Private training helps you improve faster with personal feedback. 90-minute morning sessions from 700 THB.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Private Training Leads. Target boxing/Muay Thai/fitness/personal training interests. Objective: Messages. Schedule CTA around morning availability.

## CTA

Send “PRIVATE” to book a morning slot.


## Source facts to keep consistent

- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class pricing: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is 90 minutes and available in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

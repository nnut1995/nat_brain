# 06 — No Experience? Start Here

## Content brief

- Week: Week 2
- Goal: Beginner conversion
- Format: Reel, 20–30 seconds
- Target audience: First-timers and nervous beginners
- Main offer: 400 THB first/regular session
- Hook: No experience? You can still start Muay Thai.

## Concept

Make beginners feel safe and welcome.

## What to shoot / create

- Trainer smiling/welcoming.
- Beginner wrapping hands or entering gym.
- Simple stance/guard teaching.
- Light pad work, not intense sparring.
- End card: 400 THB, beginner-friendly.

## What Nat has to do

1. Feature a real beginner if possible.
2. Avoid intimidating fight footage.
3. Use captions: No experience needed / All levels welcome.
4. Answer one fear: I am not fit enough.
5. Post with a direct booking CTA.

## Caption draft

You do not need experience to start. Our trainers help you learn step by step. First regular class: 400 THB.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Nervous Beginner. Target fitness, weight loss, beginner boxing, women/men fitness segments separately if budget allows. Objective: Messages.

## CTA

Send “BEGINNER” to book your first class.


## Source facts to keep consistent

- N&P Boxing Gym / N&P Muaythai Camp is in Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class pricing: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is 90 minutes and available in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

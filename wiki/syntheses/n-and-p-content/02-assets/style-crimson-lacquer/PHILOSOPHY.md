# Crimson Lacquer

A visual movement modelled on the everyday spectacle of the Thai gym poster, the temple-fair handbill, and the Sino-Thai shrine wall. The reference is not the minimalist Western brand sheet — it is the lacquered red ground of a household shrine, the gold-leafed mondop, the pressed-tin "HOT" sticker on a noodle-shop banner, and the lottery-poster sunburst that promises a number is about to hit. The work must look loud, generous, and lucky. Restraint is the wrong instinct here. The wall the poster will hang on is busy; the poster has to win that wall.

The field is always a deep auspicious crimson, never neutral. Treat the background as if it were red shellac applied in eight coats over a marble slab — a worked, lacquered surface with subtle veining, faint warmer pools where light might catch, and a soft grain throughout. Never a flat fill. The crimson must feel hand-painted, slightly uneven, slightly sacred, slightly vulgar — the same red used on temple gates, fight-night banners, and red-envelope foil. Negative space is not minimalism; it is breath between ornaments.

Gold is the second voice and it speaks constantly. It arrives as a high-energy sunburst in one corner — sharp triangular rays of warm yellow over a small bright sun, like a paper firework — and as filigree fleurons threaded along thin gold rules between sections. Gold also carries the headline: a bold display word set in saturated yellow with a hairline darker outline so it pops against the crimson the way temple gilt pops against red lacquer. Where the source image whispers, this aesthetic shouts; where the source image shouts, this aesthetic sings.

Information is housed in **white pill cards** — softly rounded ivory rectangles, lightly cushioned with a faint shadow, edged in a thin gold or maroon hairline. Inside the pills, type is dark, dense, and practical: pricing rows, time blocks, what-you-get bullets. Pills cluster in vertical columns with generous gutters; each pill is a small banquet table on the red ground. The pills must look stamped, not floated — flat-finished, slightly inset, like a real printed sticker that has been laid onto the red.

The piece earns its energy from a small inventory of **decorative actors** that recur across the series: the corner sunburst (always upper-right, sometimes mirrored at lower-left); the gold fleuron divider (a small four-petal diamond between two thin gold rules, used to separate sections); the **HOT! flame badge** (a jagged red-orange starburst with a slight rotation, used to flag the deal of the moment, never more than one per pill); the contact strip at the foot (phone, IG/TikTok handle, LINE QR, a yellow capsule with a one-line motivational tagline). These actors are drawn with the meticulous precision of a sign-painter who has rendered the same eight-petal flower a thousand times — every curve known, every spacing internalised, every edge clean. The work must read as the output of a craftsman at the absolute top of their trade, who has spent decades quietly perfecting exactly this kind of poster.

Typography is loud where it must be and obedient everywhere else. The **headline** is a bold display set in gold with a thin dark stroke — Thai display fonts (Sukhumvit Set Bold, Mitr, Prompt Black, Bai Jamjuree Bold) when the message is in Thai, condensed athletic display (Big Shoulders Bold, Boldonse, Tektur) when in English. **Section heads** sit on the crimson in clean cream sans, never larger than the headline, never timid. **Body** type lives inside the white pills in a workhorse sans — clear, kerned, comfortable to scan. **Numerals** — prices, times, durations — get the most weight: tabular, bold, never thin. Thai numerals or Western numerals are both fine, but mix only with intention. Every typographic choice must read as deliberate: the result of countless tiny adjustments by a designer who has set this kind of poster many times before and knows the exact balance at which the wall stops feeling cluttered and starts feeling generous.

Rhythm is high tempo and visually generous. A single composition routinely contains: one giant headline, two-to-four pill clusters, three-to-six gold ornament dividers, one or two HOT! badges, a corner sunburst, a foot strip with logo and contact, and a yellow capsule of warm copy. This is a lot — and that is the point. Restraint is the wrong instinct: the design should look like the gym has more to offer than the wall has room for. Every element, however, must be drawn with absolute craftsmanship. No element is permitted to look thrown together, AI-generated, or amateur. Every shadow soft, every corner rounded with intention, every gold rule hairline-thin, every burst symmetric, every pill aligned to a grid that the eye can feel but never quite see. The piece must look as if it were the work of a master flyer-maker on Rama 2 who has been drawing fight cards since 2009 and would be insulted if a single ray of the sunburst sat at the wrong angle.

## Quick reference — palette, type, motifs

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `LACQUER` | `#7A0B16` | base crimson field |
| `LACQUER_DEEP` | `#5A0710` | vignette pool |
| `LACQUER_HIGH` | `#9C1A26` | warm pool / highlight band |
| `GOLD_HOT` | `#F2C53D` | sunburst, headline fill |
| `GOLD_DEEP` | `#C9952A` | sunburst inner / ornament strokes |
| `IVORY_CARD` | `#F6EFE0` | pill card fill |
| `IVORY_LINE` | `#E8DCC2` | pill inner edge |
| `INK_BODY` | `#2A1010` | text inside pills |
| `FLAME_HOT` | `#E94C20` | HOT! badge fill |
| `FLAME_EDGE` | `#7A1A06` | HOT! badge stroke |
| `CAPSULE_YELLOW` | `#F4D26B` | bottom motivational pill |

**Headlines (Thai)**: Sukhumvit Set Bold, fallback Thonburi Bold; **Headlines (Latin)**: Big Shoulders Bold, Boldonse.
**Body inside pills**: Outfit Bold for labels, IBM Plex Sans / Outfit Regular for values, GeistMono for prices.
**Section heads on crimson**: Outfit Bold, ivory.

**Recurring actors**:
1. Corner sunburst (top-right, sometimes mirrored bottom-left).
2. Gold fleuron divider — `── ❖ ──` style, hairline rule + 4-petal diamond.
3. White pill card — rounded rect, soft shadow, hairline gold edge.
4. HOT! flame badge — jagged starburst, slight rotation, max one per pill.
5. Logo lockup — boxing-ring icon + stars + Thai-script gym name, top-left.
6. Yellow contact capsule — bottom strip with phone, IG/TikTok, LINE QR.
7. Marble veining — faint white wisps over the crimson, low opacity.

# 20 — Advanced and Pro Fighter Prep

## Content brief

- Week: Week 4
- Goal: High-intent authority
- Format: Reel, 20–40 seconds
- Target audience: Serious fighters, advanced trainees, martial arts travelers
- Main offer: Staycation/pro program/private training
- Hook: Preparing for the ring? Train with purpose.

## Concept

Show that N&P can serve serious trainees, not only casual fitness students.

## What to shoot / create

- Intense pad work.
- Clinch drill.
- Conditioning/roadwork if available.
- Trainer giving tactical advice.
- End card: staycation/private inquiries.

## What Nat has to do

1. Film strong but safe pro-style training.
2. Use captions about discipline, timing, clinch, fight IQ.
3. Avoid claims about specific fighter credentials unless confirmed.
4. Post to build authority.
5. Use as ad only for combat-sports audience.

## Caption draft

For serious trainees: intense pad work, clinching, conditioning, and fight IQ development in Rama 2, Bangkok.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Serious Fight Camp. Target Muay Thai fighters, MMA, combat sports, Thailand travel. Objective: Messages. Route leads to staycation or private package.

## CTA

Message us about pro-style training options.


## Source facts to keep consistent

- N&P Boxing Gym / N&P Muaythai Camp is in Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class pricing: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is 90 minutes and available in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

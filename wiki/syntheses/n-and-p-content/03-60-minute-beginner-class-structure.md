# 03 — 60-Minute Beginner Class Structure

## Content brief

- Week: Week 1
- Goal: Beginner confidence
- Format: Reel or carousel
- Target audience: Beginners who feel nervous or unsure
- Main offer: 60-minute regular class
- Hook: Here is exactly what happens in a 60-minute class.

## Concept

Reduce anxiety by showing the class structure: warm-up, pad work, weights/conditioning.

## What to shoot / create

- Clip of warm-up or shadowboxing.
- Clip of pad work.
- Clip of weights/conditioning.
- Text card with 15 min / 30 min / 15 min.
- End card: classes hourly 14:00–19:00.

## What Nat has to do

1. Record one clip from each class section.
2. Add simple labels: Warm-up, Pad Work, Conditioning.
3. Keep it educational, not intimidating.
4. Mention beginner-friendly in the caption.
5. Post before launching beginner ads.

## Caption draft

Your first class is simple: warm up, hit pads, finish with conditioning. Beginner-friendly and open to all levels.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Beginner Education. Target local fitness beginners and people interested in boxing/Muay Thai. Objective: Video Views first, then retarget viewers with message ad.

## CTA

DM “FIRST CLASS” to book.

# 11 — Eat. Sleep. Train. Repeat.

## Content brief

- Week: Week 3
- Goal: Staycation awareness
- Format: Reel, 15–30 seconds
- Target audience: Tourists, expats, digital nomads, serious trainees
- Main offer: Staycation from 1,000 THB/day
- Hook: Eat. Sleep. Train. Repeat. In Bangkok.

## Concept

Introduce the staycation lifestyle in one emotional montage.

## What to shoot / create

- Morning training.
- Food/breakfast or dinner.
- Accommodation/rest shot.
- Evening training.
- Text card: 1,000 THB/day, 25,000 THB/month.

## What Nat has to do

1. Shoot one full day or simulate a day sequence.
2. Use cinematic pacing, not hard-sell at first.
3. Overlay the four words: Eat / Sleep / Train / Repeat.
4. Mention inclusions in caption.
5. Run as top-of-funnel staycation ad.

## Caption draft

Live the Muay Thai lifestyle in Rama 2, Bangkok: 2x training per day, on-site accommodation, daily breakfast and dinner.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Staycation Awareness. Target Thailand travelers, expats, digital nomads, Muay Thai fans, English speakers. Objective: Video Views or Messages. Budget 300–600 THB/day.

## CTA

Message us for staycation availability.


## Source facts to keep consistent

- N&P Boxing Gym / N&P Muaythai Camp is in Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular classes are 60 minutes and start hourly from 14:00 to 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class pricing: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is 90 minutes and available in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private pricing: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Staycation pricing: 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; website copy lists 2x training/day, on-site accommodation, and daily breakfast/dinner ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

# 10 — Student Learning Basic Strikes

## Content brief

- Week: Week 2
- Goal: Relatability
- Format: Reel, 15–25 seconds
- Target audience: Beginners who want to see people like them
- Main offer: Beginner class / private training
- Hook: Everyone starts with the basics.

## Concept

Show a student learning stance, jab, cross, kick, or guard correction.

## What to shoot / create

- Trainer demonstrating slowly.
- Student trying the movement.
- Trainer correcting kindly.
- Student improves on second attempt.
- End card: beginner-friendly.

## What Nat has to do

1. Ask student permission before filming.
2. Show improvement, not perfection.
3. Use text overlay: Step by step.
4. Avoid making the student look bad.
5. Post as trust-building content.

## Caption draft

Muay Thai starts with fundamentals. Our trainers help you build technique step by step.

## Ad group for this content

Ad set: Beginner Trust. Use for warm retargeting or organic; boost only if comments/saves are strong.

## CTA

Want to learn step by step? DM us.

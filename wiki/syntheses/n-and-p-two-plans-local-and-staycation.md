# N&P — Two-Plan Customer Acquisition Strategy

> [!note] Why this page exists
> The original [[wiki/syntheses/n-and-p-boxing-gym-social-media-ads-plan]] mixes two audiences. This page splits the work into two clean, parallel plans so each one has its own audience, language, channels, content, and ads.

---

## Plan 1 — Local people taking normal class

### Audience

- Thai locals living/working within ~5–10 km of Rama 2.
- Fitness/weight-loss beginners.
- After-work office workers (Rama 2, Bang Khun Thian, Tha Kham, Phra Pradaeng).
- University students.
- Women who want a beginner-friendly, safe environment.
- Serious local Thai trainees (some).

### Positioning

> "เรียนมวยไทยใกล้พระราม 2 — เริ่มที่ 400 บาท/คลาส เทรนเนอร์มืออาชีพ บรรยากาศเป็นกันเอง"
> ("Real Muay Thai near Rama 2 — from 400 THB/class. Pro trainers, friendly vibe.")

Confirmed offer facts ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]):
- Regular class 60 min, hourly slots 14:00–19:00.
- 1 session 400 / 10 sessions 3,500 / 30 sessions 9,000 THB.
- Monthly unlimited group: 3,990 / 9,990 / 17,990 / 29,990 THB.
- Private 90 min, 07:00–12:00. 700 / 5,990 / 14,990 THB.
- Closed 15th & 16th monthly.

### Channels (in priority order)

1. **TikTok (Thai)** — primary discovery for Thai audience.
2. **Facebook page + Reels** — already established as `pnboxing` ([[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).
3. **Instagram Reels** — secondary.
4. **Google Maps** — local SEO; reviews + photo posts.
5. **Line Official Account** — booking + retention; broadcast offers.
6. **Meta Ads (FB/IG)** — radius targeting Rama 2.

### Language rule

All Plan 1 captions, ad copy, and Line replies in **Thai first**, English second (or omitted on TikTok).

### Ad funnel (Plan 1)

- **Campaign 1A — Local beginner trial** — Messages objective; 3–8 km radius around Rama 2; interests Muay Thai/boxing/fitness/weight-loss; budget 300–500 THB/day × 7 days; offer 400 THB single class.
- **Campaign 1B — Private morning training** — Messages; interests personal training, fitness; offer 700 THB private; angle "เทรนตัวต่อตัวช่วงเช้า ก่อนไปทำงาน".
- **Campaign 1C — Monthly unlimited (retention)** — Retargeting people who messaged or attended ≥1 class; offer 3,990 THB/month unlimited.
- **Campaign 1D — Local retargeting** — People who watched ≥50% of any video; offer stack (400 / 700 / 3,990).

#### New content to create — Plan 1

Each item below is a content brief. Format: **what to shoot → text on screen → caption → where to post → goal**.

**L1. After-work class promo (TikTok + Reels)**
- Shoot: trainer + student starting class at 18:00; clock on wall; smiling exhausted face after class.
- Text on screen: "เลิกงาน 6 โมง? คลาส 18:00 รออยู่"
- Caption (TH): "เลิกงานแล้วยังไม่อยากกลับบ้านนิ่ง ๆ มาต่อยมวยใกล้พระราม 2 — คลาส 18:00 และ 19:00 ทุกวัน เริ่มต้น 400 บาท ทักไลน์ @ppaoin"
- Post: TikTok (primary), FB Reel, IG Reel.
- Goal: capture commuter beginners.

**L2. Weight-loss / calorie burn angle (Reel)**
- Shoot: timer countdown, sweat shots, weigh-in or stat overlay (kcal estimate).
- Text on screen: "1 คลาส = ~600 kcal"
- Caption (TH): "อยากลดน้ำหนัก แต่เบื่อวิ่งลู่? คลาสมวยไทย 60 นาทีที่ N&P เผาผลาญได้ดี + ได้ทักษะการป้องกันตัว"
- Goal: hook fitness/weight-loss audience.

**L3. Women-friendly / first-time-woman POV (Reel)**
- Shoot: female student arriving, taping hands, hitting pads with female-friendly trainer; supportive vibe.
- Text on screen: "ครั้งแรกของผู้หญิงที่ N&P"
- Caption (TH): "ไม่ต้องกลัว — เทรนเนอร์ใจดี ค่อย ๆ สอน เริ่มจากศูนย์ได้"
- Goal: lower fear barrier for women.

**L4. Google Maps photo post (weekly cadence)**
- Shoot: 5 fresh photos per month — gym interior, equipment, schedule board, group class shot, exterior.
- Caption: short bilingual.
- Post: Google Business Profile.
- Goal: rank in "Muay Thai near me" searches.

**L5. Google Maps review push (Story + Line OA)**
- Asset: a printable QR card at front desk + a Story prompting current students to leave a review.
- Goal: 10+ new reviews in 30 days.

**L6. Line OA broadcast — first-time discount (one-shot)**
- Asset: image of class + offer "เพิ่มเพื่อน Line @ppaoin วันนี้ รับส่วนลดคลาสแรก".
- Goal: build Line OA audience as owned channel.

**L7. Friend-bring-friend referral (carousel)**
- Slide 1: "ชวนเพื่อนมาต่อย ได้ทั้งคู่"
- Slide 2: rules (e.g. friend gets 1st class free, you get 1 free class on their package purchase).
- Goal: viral within local network.
- Caveat: confirm with gym owner before posting — see [[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]] for what is locked vs. negotiable.

**L8. Trainer introduction series (3 reels)**
- For each trainer: 20s — name, fight record, what they're known for, why they teach.
- Caption (TH): include their nickname + record.
- Goal: humanize, build trust.

**L9. Student transformation / 30-day journey (Reel)**
- Shoot before/after pad work clips at day 1 and day 30 of the same student (with consent).
- Caption: brief stats — weight, skill milestone.
- Goal: social proof for monthly unlimited package.

**L10. "Map to gym" Story / Reel**
- Shoot: short walking POV from BTS / nearest landmark / parking to the door.
- Text on screen: directions and parking note.
- Goal: kill "I don't know where it is" objection.

**L11. Schedule poster (static, pin)**
- Asset: a clean Thai-language schedule graphic — class times 14:00–19:00, monthly closure 15th/16th, contact.
- Goal: pinned at top of FB page; reused in Line replies.

**L12. Class atmosphere Reel (no music, sound on)**
- Shoot: 20s of pad sounds, kiap shouts, trainer counting; raw audio.
- Caption (TH): "เปิดเสียงฟัง"
- Goal: TikTok algorithmic boost via authentic audio.

- [ ] **Week 4 (retention + referral)** — publish 18, 20, L7 referral, L9 transformation, L10 map; launch Campaign 1C monthly unlimited retargeting.
- [ ] **Always-on** — reply to every message <2h; track cost per message and message-to-attendance.

## Plan 2 — Staycation foreigner

### Audience

- Tourists already in or planning a Thailand trip.
- Expats living in Bangkok / SE Asia.
- Digital nomads (Chiang Mai/Bali/Bangkok circuit).
- Martial-arts travelers comparing camps (Phuket/Chiang Mai/Bangkok).
- Western fitness enthusiasts treating training as a "transformation trip".
- Returning Muay Thai students who want a Bangkok-based camp.

### Positioning

> "Train Muay Thai in Bangkok with professional fighters. Eat. Sleep. Train. Repeat. From 1,000 THB/day."

Confirmed offer facts ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]):
- 2× fighter-led training per day.
- On-site accommodation.
- Daily breakfast and dinner.
- 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month.
- Rama 2, Bangkok.

> [!warning] Caveats — do not overpromise
> Booking rules and whether every inclusion applies identically to daily vs. monthly are not fully captured in vault sources. Use "message us to confirm availability" rather than guarantee. See [[wiki/syntheses/n-and-p-boxing-gym-social-media-ads-plan]] §"Important caveats".

### Channels (in priority order)

1. **Instagram (English)** — primary; travel + martial arts hashtags.
2. **YouTube Shorts + 1 long-form vlog** — discovery via "Bangkok muay thai" searches.
3. **TikTok (English)** — secondary travel discovery.
4. **Reddit** — r/MuayThai, r/Thailand, r/digitalnomad organic posts (no spam).
5. **Google Maps + TripAdvisor** — reviews matter heavily.
6. **Meta Ads (worldwide English)** — broad-audience message ads.
7. **Booking platforms / partner referrals** — Bangkok hostels, Airbnb hosts, expat communities.

### Language rule

All Plan 2 content **English-first**. Bilingual is acceptable but the hook line must be English.

### Ad funnel (Plan 2)

- **Campaign 2A — Staycation discovery (worldwide)** — Messages objective; broad targeting; interests Muay Thai/MMA/Thailand travel/martial arts; English-language video creative; 500–800 THB/day × 14 days; offer 1,000 THB/day.
- **Campaign 2B — In-Thailand tourist** — Geo-target Thailand (excluding Rama 2 locals); travelers + expats interest; offer "Spend 3 days at a real Bangkok Muay Thai camp".
- **Campaign 2C — Digital nomad / monthly** — Target SEA digital-nomad interests; offer 25,000 THB/month.
- **Campaign 2D — Staycation retargeting** — Anyone who watched ≥75% of staycation content or visited the website; carousel of inclusions; "DM to book your dates".

#### New content to create — Plan 2

**S1. Day-in-the-life vlog (YouTube long-form, 6–10 min)**
- Shoot: 06:30 wake → morning run/training → breakfast → rest → afternoon training → dinner → evening hangout.
- Voiceover (English): a guest narrates the day.
- Goal: rank for "Muay Thai camp Bangkok" YouTube searches; cuts into 4–6 Shorts/Reels.

**S2. Day-in-the-life Reel (60–90s vertical cut from S1)**
- Caption: "What 24 hours at a Bangkok Muay Thai camp looks like."
- Goal: top-of-funnel awareness for travelers.

**S3. Bangkok vs Phuket vs Chiang Mai (carousel)**
- Slide 1: "Where should you train Muay Thai in Thailand?"
- Slides 2–4: honest pros/cons of each city.
- Slide 5: "If you want city + training, Bangkok wins. Train at N&P Rama 2."
- Goal: capture comparison-stage searchers.

**S4. "What's included" 30-second Reel**
- Visuals: training, room, breakfast, dinner — 4 quick cuts with text overlay.
- Caption: "Everything in your stay at N&P Bangkok Muay Thai Camp."
- Goal: kill ambiguity around inclusions.

**S5. Accommodation walkthrough (Reel 30s)**
- Shoot: 5–7 second clips of room, bed, bathroom, communal area, gym view.
- Caption: "Your room at N&P Muay Thai Camp."
- Goal: critical for booking confidence — foreigners need to *see* the room.

**S6. Food / breakfast & dinner reel**
- Shoot: morning Thai breakfast, post-training dinner, tropical fruit.
- Caption: "Breakfast and dinner are included. Real Thai food."
- Goal: lifestyle hook.

**S7. Foreign student testimonial (Reel + reused as ad)**
- Shoot: 30–60s English testimonial — name, country, how long they've trained, why they came to N&P.
- Get on-camera consent + permission to use as ad.
- Goal: highest-converting ad creative for Campaign 2A.
- Target: 1 testimonial/month minimum.

**S8. "How to get to N&P from Suvarnabhumi/BKK airports" (Reel)**
- Shoot: airport → taxi/Grab → arrival at gym; show approximate cost and time.
- Caption: "Getting to N&P Muay Thai Camp from BKK or DMK airport."
- Goal: kill the logistics objection.

**S9. Visa / 30-day stay friendly post (carousel)**
- Slide 1: "Most countries get 30 days visa-free in Thailand."
- Slide 2: 25,000 THB monthly fits inside that window.
- Slide 3: what you get for the month.
- Disclaimer slide: "Always check official Thai immigration for your country."
- Goal: nudge digital nomads toward monthly.

**S10. Reddit organic post — r/MuayThai (text + photos)**
- Title: "Anyone trained at N&P / P.Paoin in Bangkok? My experience after [N] sessions"
- Body: honest first-person account, photos, no aggressive sell.
- Goal: long-tail organic discovery; works only if authentic. *Plan owner posts, not gym account.*
- Caveat: ask Nat first — review subreddit rules; label as own affiliation if posting as gym.

**S11. TripAdvisor / Google Maps review push (English)**
- Asset: English review-request card given to every staycation guest at checkout.
- Asset: Story prompting recent guests to leave English review with QR.
- Goal: 5+ English reviews on Google Maps within 90 days.

**S12. Instagram travel hashtag stack (always-on)**
- Standard hashtag set per post: `#bangkokmuaythai #muaythailand #thailandtraining #digitalnomadbangkok #travelfit #muaythaicamp #bangkokfitness`.
- Goal: small but compounding Instagram discovery.

**S13. Influencer / fighter collab outreach (1× per month)**
- Identify 5 Western Muay Thai influencers (10k–100k followers) traveling in SEA each month.
- Offer: 3 nights free in exchange for 1 reel + 1 carousel + tagged story.
- Goal: 1 successful collab per quarter = high-quality UGC.

**S14. Booking-confirmation flow (DM templates + email)**
- DM template: ask preferred dates, number of guests, fitness level.
- Confirmation email/PDF: address, what to bring (gloves, shorts, hand wraps, water bottle), house rules, contact.
- Goal: smooth friction once a foreigner asks "how do I book?".

**S15. Bangkok partner-network outreach (one-time, recurring follow-up)**
- Asset: a short PDF flyer in English to email/Line to: 5 Bangkok hostels, 5 Bangkok Airbnb superhosts, 2–3 Bangkok expat Facebook group admins, 1–2 Muay Thai equipment shops.
- Offer: referral commission or free trial class for partners' guests.
- Goal: external lead source independent of paid ads.

**S16. "Train like a champion. Live like a local." brand reel**
- Shoot: split screen — training + Rama 2 street life (street food, motorbikes, evening market).
- Caption: brand line + 1 sentence.
- Goal: brand-equity reel; pin to IG profile.

#### Plan 2 — 30-day execution checklist

- [ ] **Week 1 (assets)** — Day 1 confirm staycation booking rules with gym owner (kill caveat); Day 2–3 shoot S1 day-in-the-life with a current guest (camera operator + guest needed); Day 4 cut S2/S4/S5/S6 from S1 footage; Day 5 publish 11 + S2; weekend pin S16 + 15.
- [ ] **Week 2 (proof + clarity)** — publish 12, 13, 14, S3 city comparison, S5 accommodation walkthrough, S6 food reel; launch Campaign 2A worldwide message ads.
- [ ] **Week 3 (objection handling)** — publish S8 airport guide, S9 visa carousel, S4 inclusions; record S7 testimonial #1; deploy S11 review push.
- [ ] **Week 4 (distribution)** — S10 Reddit post (after Nat approval); S15 partner outreach emails; S13 first influencer outreach; launch Campaign 2D retargeting.
- [ ] **Always-on** — S12 hashtags every post; reply to every English DM <4h; collect testimonials at every checkout.

# N&P Boxing Gym — Social Media and Ads Plan

## Basis

This plan is based on the Obsidian notes for [[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], especially the operational/pricing source, official website crawl, and Facebook page preview ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], [[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).

## Core positioning

Position N&P Boxing Gym / N&P Muaythai Camp as:

> Authentic fighter-led Muay Thai training in Rama 2, Bangkok — for beginners, fitness students, serious trainees, and staycation guests.

Useful brand lines already present in the sources:

- "TRAIN LIKE A CHAMPION. LIVE LIKE A LOCAL." ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- "EAT. SLEEP. TRAIN. REPEAT." ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- "Authentic professional fighter-led training accessible to everyone." ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- The Facebook page emphasizes training for all levels, a friendly atmosphere, and experienced trainers ([[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).

## Offers to promote

### 1. Beginner / fitness group classes

- 60-minute regular class: warm-up/shadowboxing, pad work, and weights/conditioning ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], [[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).
- Price: 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, 9,000 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Daily class times: 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, and 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Best audience: Rama 2 locals, beginners, fitness/weight-loss students, after-work customers.

### 2. Private training

- 90-minute private sessions, scheduled in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Price: 700 THB for 1 session, 5,990 THB for 10 sessions, 14,990 THB for 30 sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Best audience: beginners who want personal attention, advanced trainees improving technique, people who prefer morning training.

### 3. Staycation / fight camp

- Staycation is presented as training, food, on-site accommodation, and recovery in the gym environment ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).
- Included in website copy: 2x professional fighter-led training per day, on-site accommodation, daily breakfast and dinner, Rama 2 location ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).
- Price: 1,000 THB per day or 25,000 THB per month ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).
- Best audience: tourists, expats, digital nomads, martial arts travelers, serious trainees.

## Important caveats for ad copy

- Full street address is not captured in the notes; sources only say Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).
- There is a contradiction on holiday opening: the website says open on public holidays, while Facebook showed a Songkran closure announcement for Apr 13–16 ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]]).
- The staycation copy does not fully specify booking rules or whether every listed inclusion applies identically to both daily and monthly pricing ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).
- Monthly memberships are best described as unlimited group classes unless confirmed otherwise ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).

Use phrases like "message us to confirm availability" and avoid overpromising specifics not confirmed in the sources.

## Weekly posting schedule

Post 5x per week, prioritizing short-form video.

- Monday: beginner education — What happens in your first Muay Thai class?
- Tuesday: training clip — pad work, clinch, conditioning, trainer correction.
- Wednesday: offer post — Regular class 400 THB / private 700 THB.
- Thursday: staycation content — Train twice a day + stay on-site + breakfast/dinner.
- Friday: social proof / community — student story, trainer intro, group photo.
- Weekend stories: class availability, DM to book, repost student content, behind the scenes.

- Objective: messages or leads.
- Target: people near Rama 2, Bangkok, interested in fitness, Muay Thai, boxing, weight loss, martial arts.
- Starting budget: 300–500 THB/day for 7 days.

## Recommendation

Lead with two simple offers:

1. Local Thai/Bangkok market: Muay Thai class in Rama 2 from 400 THB.
2. Tourist/expat market: Muay Thai staycation in Bangkok from 1,000 THB/day.

The 400 THB class is the lowest-friction entry point. The staycation is the higher-value product and should get dedicated English-language short-form video ads.

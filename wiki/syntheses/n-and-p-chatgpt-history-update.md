# N&P Muay Thai — knowledge recovered from ChatGPT history

## What was added
Gym-relevant owner messages, correspondence and records from five export files were extracted into separate source pages. Facts below retain their historical or proposal status. Unrelated chats and speculative ChatGPT advice are not adopted as gym policy. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-000]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-002]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])

## Training and accommodation
- **Standard Stay & Train:** 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month; AC lodging, two meals and two sessions each day alongside professional fighters. Nat explicitly accepted 12–14 days in an August 2026 reply instruction. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- **On-site comfort:** shared bathroom and no hot water. On-site Wi-Fi and private-room status were added by ChatGPT and remain unconfirmed. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- **Nearby option:** Nat can help arrange lodging next to the gym; room inclusion was confirmed for one customer's package, pending her confirmation to reserve. No total price or general upgrade entitlement was established. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- **First visit:** equipment is provided; Nat says students need sportswear. Teaching children is explicitly mentioned, but no age threshold or kids-only timetable is given. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-000]])

## Fighter pathway — an individual proposal
Nat offered one aspiring fighter 10,000 THB/month for Stay & Train and a possible evaluation over about six months. Any subsequent fighter contract would involve earnings sharing and could be accepted or declined. The fee wording is ambiguous, with no split or waiver confirmed. The prospect later deferred because of budget; this is not an enrolled fighter or a general discount. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

> [!warning]
> A historical translation request says no additional fighters are accepted, while a later individual proposal discusses a possible fighter pathway. Treat recruitment as case-specific and time-sensitive; neither establishes open recruitment today. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

## Agent sales and service administration
EXO correspondence adds a travel-agent sales channel: lessons, stadium tickets, custom shorts and wraps. Historical quotation inputs were 700 / 2,500 / 1,000 / 350 THB respectively. Advance collection for reserved services and a separate post-service billing request both appear; retain booking-specific payment terms. These messages do not prove settled invoices or a signed ongoing contract. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-002]])

## Address, facilities and brand
- Historical owner copy supplies **5 ซอยเทียนทะเล 7 แยก 5 ถนนบางขุนเทียน-ชายทะเล แขวงแสมดำ บางขุนเทียน กรุงเทพฯ** and describes open-air training, a fitness zone, supplied gloves/wraps and parking for 50 cars. The capacity claim is historical, not a fresh site inspection. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])
- Roadside brief: 80 × 150 cm portrait, strong bilingual lettering, crimson/black/white with gold, large arrow and contact details. Direction and 100 m are brief inputs to verify before printing. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-002]])
- Equipment identity: N&P Muaythai, minimal Thai-influenced lettering and black/red/gold wraps. Facebook rejected one bilingual rename; the accepted live name is not recorded. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

## Equipment and growth ideas — not completed purchases
The sample brief requests branded wraps (ask logo MOQ), two kick-pad sizes, gloves, a 5 ft sandbag and shin guards, with material options. Thailand/China OEM research, hand-wrap cost research, and a 6 × 4 m steel-frame guest room are exploratory. The Japanese booking-page pitch is an unsolicited vendor offer, not an approved project. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])

## Revenue measurement before launch

Proposed process: record source, first enquiry date, first paid visit, package payments and refunds. Calculate collected revenue within 30 days of each first ad enquiry divided by campaign spend; report immature cohorts separately. Do not count a LINE click or call as a paid purchase. Avoid counting existing organic customers or attributing the same payment to both Facebook and Google.

The Google account's observed default goals were calls/contacts; verified purchase-value tracking is not established. Website UTM parameters alone do not connect an enquiry to offline payment. Keep a manual payment/source record initially, then validate conversion tracking before revenue-based automated bidding.

Arithmetic examples, not forecasts: eight 400 THB first classes collect 3,200 THB; one 3,500 THB ten-session pass collects 3,500 THB. Against a 3,000 THB ad test those are about 1.07× and 1.17× ROAS. Pricing: [[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]]. Revenue equal to ad spend does not cover training costs; package cash receipts also carry future service costs.

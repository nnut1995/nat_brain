## Booking and payment
- Collect **name, date, time, number of people**. Staff must confirm every booking. Walk-ins are welcome, but availability is not guaranteed. Do not promise an open slot or a particular trainer before staff confirm. Customers may choose a trainer. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Pay after class by cash or bank transfer. Class packages are non-refundable. Cancellation, rescheduling and no-shows have no fee or session deduction. If the next class is waiting, a late arrival finishes at the original end time; do not promise make-up time otherwise. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- A 30-session package can be shared with one other person; ask admin to confirm applicability to the requested group/private package. Other sharing, transfer, freeze or extension requests go to admin. Extensions are discretionary. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])

## First visit, children and facilities
- Sportswear is sufficient; gloves and wraps are free to borrow. Showers and parking are free; water is sold; no lockers. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Children around ages 5–6 can attend and usually train separately. No special admin gate is required for routine children's enquiries beyond ordinary booking confirmation. Do not advertise a guaranteed separate class or a strict minimum age; refer unusual age questions to admin. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Trainers speak Thai and a little English; reception speaks English. Refer injury and health-condition enquiries to admin. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])

## Stay & Train
- **1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/30 days; minimum one week.** Beginners welcome. Includes a private AC room, Wi-Fi, two meals and two daily sessions on training days. Bathroom is outside the room; no hot water. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Training Monday–Saturday, approximately **06:00–09:00 and 15:00–18:00**. No Sunday training; meals are still provided Sundays. Check-in **14:00**, checkout **13:00**. For a specific 15th/16th residential training enquiry, ask admin to reconcile with older closure exceptions. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]], [[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Admin confirms room availability. **50% deposit to reserve; remaining balance before check-in.** Airport pickup costs extra; admin quotes the price. Ask admin about residential cancellations/refunds; do not automatically apply class-package terms. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])

> [!warning] Contradiction with older website
> Owner-confirmed Sunday closure and 13:00 checkout supersede the September 8 website's daily-training/noon-checkout wording for customer replies. The historical source remains preserved. See [[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]] and [[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]].

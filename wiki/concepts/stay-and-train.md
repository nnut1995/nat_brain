# Stay & Train

## Definition
At N&P, a residential training package combining lodging, two meals and morning/evening training alongside professional fighters. Nat's August 2026 reply instructions specify 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month and accept a 12–14-day stay. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

## Current owner-confirmed terms — 2026-09-26
- Minimum one week; 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/30 days. Beginners welcome. Includes private AC room, Wi-Fi, two meals and two sessions on training days. Bathroom outside, no hot water. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Train Monday–Saturday, approximately 06:00–09:00 and 15:00–18:00; no Sunday training, but Sunday meals continue. Check-in 14:00, checkout 13:00. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])
- Admin confirms rooms. 50% deposit reserves the room; remaining balance before check-in. Airport pickup costs extra, quoted by admin. Residential cancellation/refund rules remain unresolved. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])

> [!warning] Contradiction with September website
> [[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]] said daily training with no closure days and noon checkout. Nat's newer answers specify no Sunday training and 13:00 checkout. Use the new policy for customer replies; confirm exceptional 15th/16th residential schedules with admin. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]])

## Why it matters
It serves visitors seeking a camp routine; the regular monthly small-group membership is a different product. The prospect emails show room amenities, trip length and affordability as actual booking questions. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

## Variants / related concepts
- On-site rooms are now confirmed private with AC and Wi-Fi. Bathroom outside; shared status from older evidence was not re-confirmed. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- Nearby lodging: Nat offered to arrange it and include it in one customer's package, with confirmation needed before reservation. Do not generalize the offer or invent a price. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- Individual fighter-support proposal: 10,000 THB/month for one prospect; possible evaluation around six months and an optional fighter contract with earnings sharing. The prospect deferred. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- Gym: [[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]. Operational context: [[wiki/syntheses/n-and-p-chatgpt-history-update]].

# Gym membership and check-in

## Definition
N&P's expressed requirements for member subscriptions and attendance recording, including QR check-in linked to a service. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])

## Why it matters
Nat wants mobile administration and a member experience without installing another app, mentioning LINE LIFF. The attendance archive contains unknown IDs, duplicate entries and explicit closure days, which matter when interpreting usage. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-000]])

## Variants / related concepts
- Subscription/membership access versus attendance events: the historical rows measure entries, not unique customers or revenue. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]])
- QR hardware and LINE-based access are explored options; no selected/deployed solution is established by these requests. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])
- Related gym: [[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]; data summary: [[wiki/syntheses/n-and-p-chatgpt-history-update]].

## Open questions
Which system is actually live? How are repeat same-day entries, unknown IDs, package deductions and closure days represented? Nat's historical system/privacy questions do not answer these implementation details. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-001]], [[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-004]])

# Memex

## Definition
A hypothetical personal knowledge device proposed by [[wiki/entities/vannevar-bush]] in 1945. The Memex was envisioned as a desk-sized machine where a user stored books, records, and communications, and built **associative trails** linking related items — a private, curated, deeply cross-referenced personal library ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-llm-wiki-pattern]]).

## Why it matters
The Memex is the conceptual ancestor of the [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern]]. Bush's vision differed from what the web became: **private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves.** The unsolved problem in Bush's design was *who does the maintenance*. The LLM solves that.

## Variants / related concepts
- The associative-trail idea anticipates today's hyperlinks and Obsidian-style backlinks.
- The personal-curation emphasis distinguishes it from the public, search-engine-driven web.

# N&P Boxing Gym

## Summary
Muay Thai training gym in Rama 2, Bangkok, also operating under the names **P.Paoin Muay Thai** and **N&P Muaythai Camp**. Offers small-group classes, private instruction, and a staycation package that bundles fighter-led training, accommodation, and meals. The website confirms the brand, pricing, schedule, and contact details, while the Facebook page adds social proof and a Songkran closure announcement ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]], [[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

## Class formats

### Regular (small group) — 60 min
- 15 min — warm-up & shadow boxing
- 30 min — pad work (3–4 rounds)
- 15 min — weight training
- Hourly slots: **14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00**

### Private (1-on-1) — 90 min
- Schedulable in the morning window **07:00–12:00**

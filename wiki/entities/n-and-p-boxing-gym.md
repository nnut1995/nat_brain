# N&P Boxing Gym

## Summary
Muay Thai training gym in Rama 2, Bangkok, also operating under the names **P.Paoin Muay Thai** and **N&P Muaythai Camp**. Offers small-group classes, private instruction, and a staycation package that bundles fighter-led training, accommodation, and meals. The website confirms the brand, pricing, schedule, and contact details, while the Facebook page adds social proof and a Songkran closure announcement ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]], [[wiki/sources/pnboxing-facebook-page]], [[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-nandpmuaythai-com]]).

Published offers were rechecked on 2026-09-08. Historical packages absent from the live site are labeled below. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

## Class formats

### Regular (small group) — 60 min
- 15 min — warm-up & shadow boxing
- 30 min — pad work (3–4 rounds)
- 15 min — weight training
- Hourly slots: **14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00**

### Private (1-on-1) — 90 min
- Schedulable in the morning window **07:00–12:00**

- Regular/trial: hourly starts **14:00–19:00**, finishing by **20:00**; private lessons arranged within **07:00–12:00**. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

## Stay & Train and booking details
- Live terms: **minimum one week**, check-in **from the afternoon**, checkout **by noon**; 1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month with lodging, breakfast/dinner and two daily sessions. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Standard offer in August 2026: **1,000 THB/day or 25,000 THB/month**, with on-site AC lodging, two meals and morning/evening training alongside professional fighters; a **12–14-day stay** is acceptable. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- On-site bathrooms are **shared, without hot water**. On-site Wi-Fi and private occupancy are not explicitly owner-confirmed. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- Nearby accommodation can be arranged; Nat confirmed room inclusion for one customer's package, pending her confirmation to reserve. No general upgraded-room price is established. ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])
- One prospect received a **10,000 THB/month proposal** and possible fighter evaluation around six months, followed by an optional contract with fight-earnings sharing. The fee wording is unresolved; the prospect deferred for budget reasons. **Not a general package or guaranteed fighter contract.** ([[wiki/sources/chatgpt-history/conversations-003]])

## First-visit payment and booking
- Arrive **10 minutes early** in comfortable sportswear; gloves/wraps are **free to borrow** and also sold. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- **Cash or Thai bank transfer**; payment on arrival or after class while deciding on a package. **Credit cards are not yet accepted.** Stay & Train deposit terms still need confirmation. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Reception and training support **Thai and English**. Chinese/Japanese website options do not imply staff speak those languages. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Send an email/LINE enquiry with plan, date/time, group size and experience; include stay dates for residential training. **Booking is confirmed only after agreement with the gym.** ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

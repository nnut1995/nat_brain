# Vannevar Bush

## Summary
American engineer best known (in this wiki) for proposing the [[wiki/concepts/memex]] in his 1945 essay *"As We May Think"* — the conceptual ancestor of personal knowledge-management systems including the [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern]] ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-llm-wiki-pattern]]).

## Key facts
- Proposed the Memex in 1945 ([[wiki/sources/2026-05-02-llm-wiki-pattern]]).
- The Memex anticipated **associative trails** — what later became hyperlinks and backlinks.

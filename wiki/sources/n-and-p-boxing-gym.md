# Source: N&P Boxing Gym Operational and Pricing Overview

> Raw file: `raw/N & P Boxing gym.md` — operational summary of N&P Boxing Gym (P.Paoin Muay Thai), Rama 2.

## TL;DR
- N&P Boxing Gym (a.k.a. P.Paoin Muay Thai) offers Muay Thai training in two formats: 60-min small-group classes and 90-min private sessions.
- Three pricing tracks: per-session group packs (400–9,000฿), unlimited monthly memberships (3,990–29,990฿), and per-session private packs (700–14,990฿).
- Open daily 14:00–20:00 with morning-only privates (07:00–12:00); **closed on the 15th & 16th of every month**.

## Key claims
- Group class structure is fixed: 15 min warm-up/shadow → 30 min pad work (3–4 rounds) → 15 min weights ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private classes are only schedulable in the 07:00–12:00 morning window ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Group classes run hourly at 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00 ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Public holidays follow normal hours; only the 15th and 16th of each month are routine closures ([[wiki/sources/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).

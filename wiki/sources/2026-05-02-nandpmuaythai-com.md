# Source: N&P Muaythai Camp website

> Crawl of the public website at https://nandpmuaythai.com/.

## TL;DR
- The site presents the business as N&P Muaythai Camp in Rama 2, Bangkok, with a brand promise of authentic, fighter-led Muay Thai training.
- It offers two main products: regular classes/memberships/private training and a staycation package that bundles training, accommodation, and food.
- The website provides clear schedule, pricing, and contact information, and it says the gym remains open on public holidays.

## Key claims
- The business is branded as N&P Muaythai Camp and located in Rama 2, Bangkok ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Training is described as authentic and fighter-led, suitable for beginners through pros ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- The website promotes a staycation offering with on-site accommodation, breakfast and dinner, and two daily training sessions ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular class format is 60 minutes: 15 min warm-up/shadowboxing, 30 min pad work, 15 min weights and conditioning ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Regular classes start hourly at 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, and 19:00 ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Private training is listed as a 90-minute session, scheduled in the 07:00–12:00 window ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- The site lists a regular-class price of 400 THB for 1 session, 3,500 THB for 10 sessions, and 9,000 THB for 30 sessions; monthly unlimited access is also advertised ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- The staycation package is priced at 1,000 THB per day or 25,000 THB per month ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- Contact details shown on the website include phone 081-445-5844, Line @ppaoin, email nandpboxinggym@gmail.com, and Google Maps location link ([[wiki/entities/n-and-p-boxing-gym]]).
- The website says the gym is closed on the 15th and 16th of every month and open on public holidays.

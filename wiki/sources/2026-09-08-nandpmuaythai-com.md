# N&P website — live offers and visitor guidance, September 2026

Read the public English homepage and [Thai version](https://www.nandpmuaythai.com/th) on 2026-09-08. Their published offers and visitor rules agree on the details below. This is a dated website observation, separate from the May crawl; no raw file was created or changed.

## TL;DR
- The live offer list contains trial/group sessions, group passes, one private session, and Stay & Train; previously recorded unlimited memberships and private passes are absent.
- Stay & Train requires at least one week, runs daily without closure days, and gives approximate training and arrival/departure times.
- New visitor guidance covers free equipment loans, payment, language support, arrival time and enquiry-based booking.

## Key claims

### Published packages
| Package | THB | Duration / validity |
|---|---:|---|
| First trial | 400 | 60 minutes; 1-day validity |
| Regular single class | 400 | 60 minutes; 1-day validity |
| Regular 10-session pass | 3,500 | 3 months |

| Private single lesson | 700 | 90 minutes; 1-day validity |
| Stay & Train daily rate | 1,000/day | Minimum stay 1 week |
| Stay & Train monthly | 25,000/month | Minimum stay 1 week |

Observed in the live Plans section and enquiry selector. No unlimited group memberships or multi-session private passes appear in either reviewed language version. Absence does not prove discontinuation. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

### Schedule and Stay & Train
- Regular/trial classes begin hourly at 14:00–19:00; the final class finishes at 20:00. Private lessons are arranged within 07:00–12:00. The enquiry form explicitly closes **regular and private classes on the 15th/16th** monthly. All times are Bangkok time (UTC+7). ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Stay & Train operates **every day without closure days**, approximately **06:00–09:00 and 15:00–18:00**. These are approximate windows, not a promised exact session duration. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Stay & Train includes two daily training sessions, accommodation at the gym, breakfast and dinner. Minimum stay is **one week**; check-in is **from the afternoon**, checkout **by noon**. Exact afternoon check-in hour is unspecified. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

### Visitor guidance
- Arrive **10 minutes before class**. Beginners learn stance, movement, striking and defence. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Wear comfortable sportswear. **Gloves and wraps can be borrowed free or purchased**; sale prices are not listed. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Payment is **cash or Thai bank transfer**, on arrival or after class if still choosing a package. **Credit cards are not currently supported**; the page describes future support without a launch date. Do not infer a universal no-deposit Stay & Train policy from this class-oriented FAQ. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Reception and instruction are available in **Thai and English**. The website also exposes Chinese/Japanese language links, which do not establish Chinese/Japanese-speaking staff. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- Published address: **เลขที่ 5 ซอยเทียนทะเล 7 แยก 5 ถนนบางขุนเทียน-ชายทะเล แขวงแสมดำ เขตบางขุนเทียน กรุงเทพฯ 10150**. [Published map pin](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PRsm1knPNSi4jcKt7). Phone **081 445 5844**, LINE **@ppaoin**; site structured data lists **nandpboxinggym@gmail.com**. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

### Enquiry flow
- Visitors provide name, plan, preferred date/time, party size, experience and optional notes. Stay & Train enquiries should state check-in/out dates; mixed-experience groups should say so. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])
- The page instructs visitors to create an email in their email app or copy the enquiry into LINE and send it themselves. **The gym must agree before a booking is confirmed**; choosing a package or creating an enquiry is not confirmation. No message was sent during this review. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-08-nandpmuaythai-com]])

# N&P owner clarifications — children and class booking replies

Source: Nat's direct corrections to actual N&P Customer Pilot replies in this conversation on September 30, 2026. No raw files were created or modified.

## TL;DR
- Remove the separate-training claim from children's replies.
- For ordinary class enquiries, collect the requested booking details directly, without saying availability or the queue must be checked first.
- After a customer supplies their time, acknowledge the request, not a confirmed booking; staff handle full sessions themselves.

## Key claims
- Nat explicitly requests removal of the children's separate-training context. This does not establish that children always train together with adults; the chatbot must not invent either arrangement. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-30-n-and-p-booking-reply-clarifications]])
- Customers can submit a class booking request directly. Collect only missing name, date, time and number of people; preserve age and other details already supplied in the thread. Do not tell the customer that the bot will check availability, queue space or an open slot. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-30-n-and-p-booking-reply-clarifications]])
- When the time and other details are supplied, acknowledge and summarize the requested details without confirming the booking, reserving a place, guaranteeing capacity or claiming a staff notification/action happened. Staff respond themselves if the class is full. Keep any staff review in the internal note (`needs_admin=true`); ordinary booking review does not trigger a permanent human-takeover pause. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-30-n-and-p-booking-reply-clarifications]])
- These corrections concern ordinary class booking replies, including children. They do not waive the existing Stay & Train room-availability review, deposit rules, health routing, package exceptions or known class closures. ([[wiki/sources/2026-09-26-n-and-p-owner-chatbot-policies]], [[wiki/sources/2026-09-30-n-and-p-booking-reply-clarifications]])

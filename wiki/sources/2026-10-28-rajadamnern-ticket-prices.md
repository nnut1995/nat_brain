# Rajadamnern Stadium ticket prices (checked 2026-10-28)

## TL;DR
- Official site links each fight night to its Ticketmelon page; prices are shown there, not on rajadamnern.com itself.
- Weeknight prices: Presidential Box 3,500 · Singha Ringside 2,500 · Club Class 1,800 · LEO Section 1,500 THB.
- Saturday RWS nights price the Panoramic Balcony at 10,000 THB (4,500 on other nights) and may open Third Class at 1,000 THB.

## Key claims
| Seat | Weeknight (29 Oct 2026, Petchyindee) | Saturday RWS (31 Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Panoramic Balcony (VIP Lounge, 15 seats) | 4,500 | 10,000 (free-flow beer, gift set) |
| Couple Suite (for two) | 9,000 | 9,000 |
| Presidential Box, sections 1–2 | 3,500 | 3,500 |
| Singha Ringside, sections 3–7 | 2,500 | 2,500 |
| Club Class, sections 8–9 | 1,800 | 1,800 |
| LEO Section, section 10 (unassigned) | 1,500 | 1,500 |
| Third Class, section 11 (unassigned) | not listed | 1,000 |

- The stadium has no seat named "VIP"; VIP-style options are Presidential Box, Panoramic Balcony and Couple Suite.
- Boxes, balcony and suites include soft drinks and snacks. Prices are per seat except Couple Suite.
- Event pages: ticketmelon.com/raj/petchyindee-29oct26 and ticketmelon.com/raj/rws-31oct26, reached from https://rajadamnern.com/tickets/. Seat descriptions: https://rajadamnern.com/seats-type/.
